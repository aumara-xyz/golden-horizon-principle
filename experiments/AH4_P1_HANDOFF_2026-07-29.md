## 2. The design finding

Two recoverability experiments have run and both returned negative:

- **GH-RECOV** (2026-07-03): golden vs silver vs bronze *spreading constants*, fixed
  generic structure. Silver optimal, golden 0/16 on the adversarial tear.
- **K-RECOV-001** (2026-07-19, external): golden vs silver vs bronze vs exp-2 vs
  greedy-rank vs uniform *allocation schemes*, fixed generic structure. Heavy tails
  beat uniform by +15 points; golden indistinguishable from greedy at +0.006.

Both asked **is the number φ special**. The answer is no, twice, from two
laboratories, and this preregistration does not re-ask it.

Neither asked **is the fusion structure special**. No Fibonacci fusion tree has been
built in this programme. That is the ledger's own AH.4-P1 row and the short paper's
§8, and it is the axis the new design varies.

The prereg is therefore a **4 × 4 factorial, 16 cells**: four fusion categories
(Fibonacci, Ising, SU(2)₄, Z₃ abelian) crossed with four allocation constants
(uniform, golden, silver, bronze), the latter retained only as a control since two
experiments say it is inert. The prior tests varied the constant factor and held
structure fixed.

Two corrections from external review are folded in. The classical code is **not** a
level of the structure factor: it has no fusion-tree basis, so including it would
require the bespoke code path the design forbids. It is now a separately reported
floor that may enter no contrast. And SU(2)₄ (dimension √3) was added so the
structure factor has an interior point, which is what makes the monotonicity kill
at §2.3 detectable rather than a two-point difference open to either reading.

**Why its pass-region excludes the generic answer.** The generic result, that any
heavy-tailed allocation beats uniform, lives entirely on the constant axis and is
now baseline rather than finding. The discriminating claim is about the structural
axis with the constant held fixed, and *flatness in that axis is the pre-registered
falsifier*. This is what makes the test admissible under the standing rule in §8,
that no new compute be spent on a test whose pass-region contains the standard
answer. The same rule is why the DMRG band should receive no new compute: its band
contains β = 8/9.

## 3. What the pentagon gate establishes, and what it does not

The gate verifies that the F-symbols for all three categories satisfy the pentagon
identity, which is the consistency condition on an associator. Results:

| Category | Non-trivial index assignments | max abs residual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fibonacci | 48 | 1.110e-16 | PASS |
| Ising | 132 | 2.220e-16 | PASS |
| Z₃ abelian | 81 | 0.000e+00 | PASS |

**This is not evidence for GHP and cannot become evidence for GHP.** It is verified
computation of published category theory (Fibonacci F-symbols per Kitaev 2006
appendix E; Ising per Bonderson 2007 §2). Both outcomes were uninformative about the
conjecture in advance. It exists for one reason: a recovery fidelity computed in a
basis whose associativity data is wrong is a number about nothing, and this check is
seconds of compute against a build measured in days.

**The forced quantum dimensions are worth noting for the numerology tripwire.** The
gate prints them because they are *outputs* of the fusion rules, never inputs:
Fibonacci gives d = φ because τ ⊗ τ = 1 ⊕ τ forces d² = d + 1; Ising gives d = √2
because its rule forces d² = 2. This is the structural sense in which the experiment
compares a φ-capacity object against a √2-capacity object, with neither constant set
by hand. It is the material difference from the two prior tests, where the constant
was a dial someone chose.

No claim is attached to that observation. Capacity is not a rate and not a
mechanism, and a higher quantum dimension does not entail better recovery. That
entailment is the thing the experiment exists to measure rather than assume.

### 3.1 Gate 1, and the confound it found in our own design

`ah4_p1_braid_gate.py` was written to check a claim the first draft of this handoff
asserted from the literature without verifying: that Fibonacci anyons are universal
for quantum computation by braiding alone and Ising anyons are not. Counting the
braid-group image on three strands up to global phase, both categories give
identical counts for the first three word lengths and then separate. **Ising closes
at exactly 24 elements**, which is the order of the single-qubit Clifford group
modulo phase, consistent with the textbook Clifford-only result and its
Gottesman-Knill simulability. **Fibonacci does not close**, still growing at 69,900.
Claim reproduced.

**This exposed a confound in the design as first filed.** If the Fibonacci arm
recovers better, the cause could be the fusion structure or merely a richer set of
available recovery operations, and the first draft would have reported either as
"structure carries recoverability." Prereg §2.4 now fixes a **matched operation
budget** across arms, so the two explanations separate: favoured at matched budget
means structural, favoured only at unbounded budget means operational richness,
which is a real result under a different name and not the Layer-3 payout.

Recorded here rather than quietly patched, because a design defect found by one's
own gate before signing is the gate working.

### 3.2 A standing guard against a recurring question

`quantum_dimension_admissibility.py` records the Jones (1983) index bound
mechanically. Dimensions in the discrete series are 2cos(π/n), strictly below 2, so
of the metallic means only gold is admissible, and φ is the unique member of both
the metallic and the Chebyshev families. There is no silver or bronze category to
add as a further arm. It also pins a correction worth keeping visible: Ising's
dimension √2 ≈ 1.414 is **not** the silver mean 1+√2 ≈ 2.414. Those two numbers
share a continued-fraction tail, which matters for approximability and nothing
else.

Committed because the question arrives repeatedly in different costumes, and a
mechanical answer costs less than re-deriving it each time.

## 5. A separate finding: the SYK gate is blocked on work that needs no compute

Not part of this branch. Recorded here because it is the highest-value item found
while reading the ledger, and because it is time-ordered.

`P-002a` is **NEVER RUN**, and its blockers are a repaired pipeline, a completed
N=22, and a written ν→β conversion protocol satisfying **OP 179**. The first two
need compute. The third does not.

OP 179 is defined in `GHP_v1_618_MASTER.md` (AE.10): derive the operational
procedure by which β_fusion is extracted from a finite system, specifying observable,
data pipeline and fit protocol **before any experimental data is allowed to inform
the definition**. `OP 111`, the converter and bucket logic including the
quotient-confirmation branch, is separately logged as specified but never coded.

**Why this is urgent rather than merely outstanding.** AE.8 and AE.9 record that the
channel-exponent assignment is *non-unique*: the logged candidates are 1.236, 1.328,
1.412 and 1.764, spread across a band of [0.618, 1.618]. With four admissible
assignments available and a band that wide, almost any measured exponent can be
matched after the fact by choosing the assignment that fits. That is precisely the
failure OP 179's before-data clause exists to prevent, and it means the converter
is only legitimate if written first. The window for doing this honestly closes the
moment anyone runs the N=22 job.

**What this lane can and cannot contribute.** The decision-bucket logic is
deterministic bookkeeping, is testable against synthetic inputs before real data
exists, and is squarely the kind of thing the symbiote lane builds. The ν→β
assignment itself is GHP physics and is not ours to choose; picking among those four
candidates is exactly the judgement OP 179 reserves. The offer is to build the
buckets with the assignment as an explicit injected port that this lane fills, so
the verdict becomes mechanical while the physics decision stays where it belongs.

## 6. What this branch does not claim

In the idiom of `DO_NOT_CLAIM.md`, and to be read as an extension of it for the
duration of AH.4-P1:

- Not that the pentagon gate passing is evidence for GHP, Fibonacci selection, or
  the observer-boundary conjecture. It is verified textbook computation.
- Not that a positive AH.4-P1 result would prove physical selection of φ. It would
  show a recoverability difference between fusion categories in a simulated code,
  which is engineering evidence about an architecture.
- Not that the quantum-dimension observation in §3 supports anything. It is a
  restatement of the fusion rules.
- Not that the deformation-pass provenance in §4 constitutes independent
  confirmation of the architecture-versus-dynamics thesis. Two methods agreeing is
  not two experiments agreeing.
- Not that anything here bears on the SYK corridor, the DMRG band, or the closed
  dynamical-selection lane, each of which is separately governed.

## 7. Open questions for the lane before the prereg is locked

These are decisions this lane should not make unilaterally:

1. **Is Ising the right primary structural control?** It is chosen as the fairest
   available: non-abelian, modular, physically realistic, different fusion algebra,
   which isolates *Fibonacci* rather than merely *non-abelian*. A different MTC may
   be preferred.
2. **Is ±0.02 the right margin?** Carried from K-RECOV-001 for cross-experiment
   comparability rather than derived from a power calculation for this design.
3. **Does the erasure model match GH-RECOV closely enough** for the three results to
   be read against each other, or should the burst/tear stressor be specified more
   tightly?
4. **Who supplies the recovery routine?** This is the genuine build cost and it is
   physics work. The prereg names the shortcut that must be refused: approximating
   the fusion tree by a generic weighted allocation reproduces GH-RECOV exactly and
   answers nothing.
5. **Signing.** `date_locked` is deliberately absent. Locking is the lane's ceremony,
   not this branch's.
