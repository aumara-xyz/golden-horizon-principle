## Sign audit

The published display after Eq. (14) says `cos(theta[p,m]) = T[p,m]/l[p,m]`. Taken literally, this contradicts the target recursion in Eqs. (11)–(12), the explicit `p=2, m=1` matrix in Eq. (9), and Supplemental Eq. (B1). At `p=2, m=1`, the printed sign produces trace `+1/sqrt(2)` where Eq. (11) requires `-1/sqrt(2)`, an absolute residual of `sqrt(2)`. The executable retains both signs and uses the uniquely consistent correction

```text
cos(theta[p,m]) = -T[p,m]/l[p,m].
```

This is an implementation correction to an internally inconsistent printed formula, not a fitted phase.

## Finite truncation

The source explicitly needs independent prime and repetition cutoffs (`p<P` and `m<M`). Round 5 fixed `P in {29,53,97,193}` but did not register `M`. The script therefore freezes `m<=8` and labels all conclusions finite. Prime cutoffs are inclusive. Traces are evaluated directly on 8,193 equally spaced points in the registered interval `0<=k<=256`. At `P=193`, `m<=6` and `m<=10` are sensitivity mutations; each reruns its own pseudo-prime control before its authentic trace comparison and repeats all three central scatterers.

The matched pseudo-prime control is computed first. It keeps the exact copy topology and directed-bond count, replaces prime logarithms by deterministic equally spaced interior logarithms, then applies one common scale to match total physical length. Its own KHR recursion is a machine-precision match, but it does not match the authentic finite prime trace. This demonstrates both facts that matter: the recursion is generic algebra, while the arithmetic frequencies select the prime trace.

## Central-scatterer surgery

For each finite swarm, `S0` is the direct sum of the published real `2x2` rotation scatterers, and propagation is `D(k)=diag(exp(i k L_j))`. The surgery is the explicit zero-length common-vertex composition

```text
S_C = C S0,
```

where `C` is the identity, the normalized positive-phase DFT matrix, or a complex Haar unitary generated by NumPy `PCG64DXSM(52025555)` plus complex-Gaussian QR. The identity exactly recovers the uncoupled swarm. DFT and Haar remain unitary but create cross-prime two-step closed walks of lengths `log(pq)` and change every tested primitive `log p` coefficient by more than one percent.

Equation (4) supplies the general complex orbit expansion. In the source's
directed-bond formalism, using the finite unitary `S_C` at the common vertex
together with the derivative condition matched as in Eq. (5) gives the
self-adjoint compact directed-bond Hamiltonian. The implementation checks the
boundary-matrix unitarity and trace coefficients; it does not independently
construct that operator domain or enumerate its eigenvalues. Mixed paths have
lengths `log(product p_i^m_i)`, so exact arithmetic length degeneracies remain
even though primitive `log p` walks still exist. Chaoticity was not tested.

The smooth density is always `L_tot/pi`; central coupling does not change the lengths and therefore cannot remove it. Across the four cutoffs, `L_tot/pi` divided by the finite prime-trace RMS increases from about `4.98e2` to `2.50e3`. Thus the Weyl term is not an algebraic cancellation mechanism: it increasingly dominates the finite oscillatory trace as this cutoff grows.
