This page renders and sonifies one object: the Riemann-Siegel main sum for Hardy's Z-function on the critical line. All of the mathematics below is standard (DLMF 25.10) and none of it is claimed as new. Every number on screen is computed from these formulas — nothing here is measured from nature, and nothing here certifies anything.

The formulas

Z(t) = eiθ(t) ζ(½ + it) (real for real t) θ(t) ≈ (t/2) ln(t/2π) − t/2 − π/8 + 1/(48t) + 7/(5760t³) (working asymptotic) θ′(t) ≈ ½ ln(t/2π) − 1/(48t²) − 7/(1920t⁴) N(t) = ⌊√(t/2π)⌋ an = 2/√n (computed spectral amplitude) φn(t) = θ(t) − t ln n cn(t) = an cos φn(t) M(t) = Σn=1..N(t) cn(t) (the main sum — everything drawn and heard) R_ref(t) = Z_ref(t) − M(t) (remainder, by subtraction from high-precision reference) tn = 2πn² (CUTOFF-ENTRY height of term n — a height, never a frequency) hn(u) = ( an cos φn(u), an sin φn(u), scale_t · (u − t₀) ) (3D phase trajectory) f_audio_n = v_t · (θ′(t) − ln n)/2π (Truth Audio law)

What is omitted

The geometry and the audio render the main sum M(t) only. The remainder R_ref(t) = Z_ref(t) − M(t) is not drawn as geometry; it is shown as a separate labeled trace where fixture data exists, and disclosed here. Labeling laws in force

• amplitudes 2/√n are "computed spectral", never "measured"
• 2πn² is a cutoff-entry height, never a frequency
• sign changes of the main sum are "computed crossings (finite approximation)"; sign changes of fixture Z_ref are "computed reference crossings"; neither is a certified zero
• the refined zero list is Odlyzko-derived and mpmath-refined, NOT certified
• heights near 10⁸ sit at the float64 phase-accuracy frontier (~10⁻⁷ rad); that is where this instrument's honest range ends

The Riemann fence

Nothing in this instrument is evidence for or against the Riemann Hypothesis. It draws a finite, classical approximation to a function whose zeros are the subject of that conjecture, and it checks its own arithmetic against published references. That is all it does. If it is beautiful, the beauty belongs to the mathematics, not to any claim.
