   term n enters the Riemann-Siegel main sum (N(t) >= n). The phrase "string birth" is
   allowed only in installation-mode copy, and even there it names the cutoff-entry event,
   not a tone.
2. **The phasor sum's imaginary part is an AUXILIARY coordinate.** Only the real part of the
   phasor sum equals the main sum M(t). The magnitude of the phasor sum is never labeled
   |Z|, and no copy may imply that the 2D phasor picture is the modulus of anything.
3. **Main-sum crossings are "computed reference crossings", never certified zeros.** This
   instrument certifies nothing. Comparison against published zero tables is a validation
   check on our computation, not a verification of the zeros themselves.
4. **Amplitudes are "computed spectral", never "measured".** Nothing here is measured from
   nature. Every number on screen is computed from known formulas.
