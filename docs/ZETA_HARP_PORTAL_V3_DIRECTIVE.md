# 2. MISTRAL HYPE TRANSLATION MATRIX

(Also standing as docs/MISTRAL_HYPE_TRANSLATION_MATRIX.md at build Gate 1.)

## Claim: "0 is the superposition."
Grounded kernel: |0> is one qutrit basis state in the computational basis. A genuine pole
superposition is |B> = (|-1> + |+1>) / sqrt(2). The same state that is a basis vector in
one basis may be represented as a superposition in another basis.
Portal: TRIT / QUTRIT / BASIS GATE.
Question: How does the description of one fixed qutrit state change when the user changes
measurement basis?

## Claim: "The Harp is a quantum simulator."
Grounded kernel: the Harp is classical deterministic arithmetic. Its main sum has an
exact finite-dimensional Hilbert-space embedding. A quantum circuit can estimate the
corresponding overlap. No quantum speedup or physical quantum simulation has been
demonstrated.
Portal: THE QUANTUM SHADOW.
Question: What are the complete state-preparation, readout, precision, and hardware costs
of estimating the Harp overlap using a real quantum circuit?

## Claim: "The zero is collapse."
Grounded kernel: a zeta or Hardy-Z zero is an analytic condition. A quantum measurement
is a stochastic readout event. These are different mathematical operations. A zero
crossing may trigger a dramatic installation effect, but it is not physical wavefunction
collapse.
Portal: ZERO / MEASUREMENT FIREWALL.
Question: Can the portal make the distinction between analytic cancellation and quantum
measurement intuitively obvious?

## Claim: "The observer collapses itself."
Grounded kernel: the observer selects the projection, basis, and observable. Different
selections reveal different readable aspects of the same mathematical object. The
underlying object does not change.
Portal: THE OBSERVER'S QUESTION.
Question: Which properties are representation-invariant, and which exist only in a chosen
projection?

## Claim: "We are doing what Flatiron did."
Grounded kernel: Flatiron used geometry-matched tensor networks to advance classical
simulation of certain quantum dynamics. The methodological lesson is that
architecture-matched representation can change computational tractability. The Harp has
not demonstrated quantum advantage or quantum-essential behavior.
Portal: THE TENSOR MIRROR.
Question: Does any natural representation of the evolving Harp trajectory compress better
than matched controls?

## Claim: "This gives a classical shortcut to RH."
Grounded kernel: no such shortcut exists. The Harp lives on the critical line. Actual
RH-sensitive finite machinery requires the full critical strip, total zero counting,
critical-line zero counting, the argument principle, and Turing-style completeness tests.
Portal: THE TESSERACT GATE + THE TURING GATE.
Question: Can a rigorous four-dimensional visualization make the distinction between
critical-line zeros and all critical-strip zeros understandable and computationally
inspectable?
