# Portal V3 addendum — build priorities from the external reviewer's tesseract explainer (adopted 2026-08-02)

Adopted by owner directive ("tune to what Codex said... keep making this zeta harp cooler").
These bind the V3 build gates, in priority order:

1. **THE DIMENSIONAL LADDER FIRST.** The portal opens: point → line → square → cube →
   tesseract → the zeta surface. Never drop a visitor into a 4D storm; let each dimension
   be built in front of them, then replace the empty tesseract with the real dataset.
   (Judged the single most important coherence feature.)
2. **SHADOW · SLICE · UNFOLD.** Three giant buttons — the three ways humans read 4D:
   the rotating 3D shadow, the 3D cross-section, the unfolded cells. Slicing rescues
   everyone the shadow loses.
3. **THE ARGUMENT LOOP BEFORE DECORATION.** The strongest mathematical unlock: draw a
   closed loop in the strip; the phase winding counts the zeros inside. "The observer
   asks a question" made into a real operation. Priority over all cosmetic rooms.
4. **SIX PLAYABLE ROTATIONS.** The six SO(4) planes on six knobs/controls, rotation
   matrix shown live, canonical-view reset, shareable saved views. The tesseract as an
   instrument, not decoration.
5. **THE REMAINDER AS A CHARACTER.** The gap between the main sum and full Hardy Z drawn
   as a golden ghost-thread that closes the gap — honest and beautiful, shrinking with
   height exactly as the validation measured.
6. **THREE EXPLANATION LEVELS.** Explorer (an 11-year-old), Student (equations + lessons),
   Research (precision, errors, controls, export, hashes) — one engine, three depths.
7. **MEASURE THE TEACHING.** A small pre/post study (portal vs ordinary plots, retention
   next day) — even without a theorem, a demonstrated learning gain is a publishable
   result on its own.
8. **SUPERCOMPUTER BEFORE QUANTUM.** GPU/CPU tiles, adaptive meshes, winding evaluation,
   streaming — the useful machine now; the quantum overlap demo is a future special
   instrument inside the portal, not its foundation.
